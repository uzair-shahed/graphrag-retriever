Why are the extraction scores low?

The scorer is arithmetically correct. The benchmark mixes genuine Qwen mistakes with an underspecified, open-label agreement task. The current scores cannot by themselves measure absolute factual extraction quality.

Answers to the six questions

Is Qwen just weak?

Real weaknesses are visible, but model capacity is not isolated.

Examples include a plant typed as an animal, nationality typed as an organization, incorrect relation arguments, missing information, and degenerate repeated triples. There is no controlled larger-model or thinking-on comparison here; quantization, decoding, annotation policy and checkpoint all matter.

Is evaluation too strict?

Strict label agreement is a major contributor.

There is no similarity cutoff to lower. Entity matches require normalized surface text AND type; relation matches require both typed endpoints AND the directed relation label. With unconstrained label vocabularies, valid paraphrases, coarse types and inverses can be counted wrong.

Is evaluation incorrect?

No arithmetic, ID alignment or frozen-reference error found.

Independent recomputation matches every stored row. Gold/candidate cohorts, fingerprint, references and source identities validate. Renaming IDs and reordering arrays preserves perfect self-agreement. The important weakness is construct validity: this measures agreement with one annotator's representation, not all supported facts.

Is gold wrong and Qwen actually good?

Gold is unreviewed model-generated silver; the reversal claim is not supported.

All gold mention strings occur in their paragraph bodies. That is reassuring grounding evidence, not semantic verification. The manual examples show both defensible Qwen alternatives and clear Qwen errors. A blind human audit is still required before estimating gold error or ranking absolute quality.

Are better evaluation methods available?

Use a suite: strict reproducible agreement plus validated semantic diagnostics.

Add surface-only and canonical-schema scores, controlled one-to-one overlap alignment, relation evaluation conditional on entity alignment, and blind source-supported fact review. Keep strict results visible. No fuzzy score, embedding similarity or LLM judge alone is a substitute for truth.

Could extraction improve?

Yes, there are testable candidates; no gain has been measured yet.

Start with clear guidelines, a fixed ontology, recorded decoding, a stronger baseline and entity-then-relation extraction. Compare specialized models and task adaptation after an independent reference and held-out development/test split exist.

Fix the checkpoint choice before testing reasoning

The current local qwen3:4b-instruct metadata says general.finetune=Instruct, general.version=2507, and links to the Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 license. Its official card identifies it as non-thinking only. Ollama's generic thinking capability is insufficient evidence that the flag changes its learned behavior. [S1] [S3]

The installed qwen3:4b instead identifies Thinking-2507 with controls [true]; comparing these two 4B tags changes checkpoint weights. The installed qwen3:8b exposes [false,true] controls and its model card documents both modes. It is the clearest already-installed starting point for a paired mode experiment. [S2] [S13]

Historical run files record model tags and preflight family/quantization, but no immutable weight digest. Current local metadata, including digests, is saved in model_identity.json; it does not retroactively prove historical identity. All local model checks were metadata-only.

musique:2hop__460946_294723:paragraph:2 · Green

For the ancient Egyptians, green had very positive associations. The hieroglyph for green represented a growing papyrus sprout, showing the close connection between green, vegetation, vigor and growth. In wall paintings, the ruler of the underworld, Osiris, was typically portrayed with a green face, because green was the symbol of good health and rebirth. Palettes of green facial makeup, made with malachite, were found in tombs. It was worn by both the living and dead, particularly around the eyes, to protect them from evil. Tombs also often contained small green amulets in the shape of scarab beetles made of malachite, which would protect and give vigor to the deceased. It also symbolized the sea, which was called the "Very Green."

Is the reference trustworthy?

It is a single-model silver reference, stored under the historical gold terminology. Structural validation verifies shapes, identity, provenance and IDs, not factual truth, consistent ontology, annotation completeness or an appropriate relation granularity. A lower score against this reference does not prove a lower utility graph.

There are 395 distinct gold type strings (167 used once) and 1,368 relation strings (876 used once), versus 134 and 811 normalized strings in Qwen. The prompt requests consistency but supplies no fixed label inventory or definitions. Gold often extracts concepts, occupations, date nodes and reified events, while Qwen sometimes emits coarser direct facts. Both are plausible representations until guidelines define the task. Gold's density alone does not prove hallucination, and Qwen's sparsity alone does not prove good precision.

A literal grounding screen found 0/3,589 gold mention occurrences absent from the body, and 18/2,062 Qwen occurrences absent even after normalization (33 fail raw substring matching). These are raw occurrence counts, not deduplicated metric denominators. Title-only NASCAR is one clear candidate violation of the copy-from-body instruction. Grounded words can still have wrong types, arguments or unsupported relations. This screen is not a semantic hallucination rate.

The seeded review does not establish that the reference is generally wrong or that Qwen performed well overall. It establishes representation ambiguity and concrete Qwen failures. Independent double annotation is the next way to answer the gold-error question. Research on DocRED shows why missing reference facts can distort evaluations, but its error rates cannot be transferred to this dataset. [S8]

Protocol weaknesses beyond the scorer

  • Mixed generation contracts: all 180 reference responses are migrated legacy outputs; Qwen has 171 legacy and 9 simplified outputs. Both original legacy source prompts match, but the current mixed candidate is not a clean single-prompt run. Comparing it with a fresh thinking-on run would add a prompt/schema confound. The two subgroups are not randomized, so their similar F1 values do not prove format has no effect.
  • Decoding differs from published guidance: current provider code sends Ollama temperature=0 and overrides the model's local 0.7 parameter. The Instruct card suggests 0.7, top_p=0.8, top_k=20. The code does not record/configure a seed or num_ctx. Exact historical wire settings for every legacy row are unavailable. Test decoding as a separate factor; do not assume it caused the errors. [S1]
  • Context is not established: maximum body length here is only 1,577 characters, making missing label policy more directly evidenced than severe source truncation. However, instructions, schema, input, generated answer and thinking share runtime constraints. Model-advertised context is not the allocated runtime window. No truncation diagnosis is established from saved data. [S12]
  • One schema failure is not the main cause: the failed paragraph refers to nonexistent target M20. The official policy makes the whole prediction empty. Even replacing it with perfect gold would move entity F1 from 37.24% to 38.45%, and relation F1 from 2.79% to 4.08%. This optimistic bound is not an observed repair.
  • Small, clustered coverage: 180 unique paragraph texts come from just 9 MuSiQue records selected as a prefix. This is not 180 independent documents. Broad claims need more diverse held-out document groups and uncertainty estimates.
  • Retried failures and temperature=0 do not create experimental replicates: reused successes plus selectively regenerated failures form the present snapshot. Record equal retry policies prospectively, retain all attempts in future runs, and compare independent full runs rather than cherry-picking best retries.

A more informative evaluation suite

  1. Keep strict normalized typed micro P/R/F1 as the archival baseline. Add per-record or macro scores so long dense paragraphs do not wholly dominate. Report raw validity, generation failures, omissions, retry counts, resource use and the exact evaluation population.
  2. Add surface-only entity matching and controlled type canonicalization. For future span annotations, report strict span/type and one-to-one overlap F1 separately. Current artifacts lack trusted offsets, so substring matching is not a true span benchmark. Fit alias/boundary rules on separate development material, freeze them and inspect precision/recall changes.
  3. Evaluate relations at multiple levels. End-to-end strict triples, relation classification on predicted/aligned entity pairs, and a clearly labeled gold-entity oracle answer different questions. Use a fixed ontology or preregistered inverse mapping; keep negation, direction, argument roles and temporal qualifications. A token/embedding match alone cannot distinguish ownership from reverse ownership. CaRB offers ideas for open-text tuple matching but is not a drop-in score for this typed graph representation. [S7]
  4. Audit source-supported facts with blinded humans. Judge each sampled predicted fact for support, argument correctness and scope; annotate missing facts independently for recall. Consider an independent LLM judge for triage only after calibrating it against human decisions. Blind model names and randomize order; document judge disagreement and self-preference risks. Published judge findings concern other tasks, not validated extraction accuracy here. [S10]
  5. Quantify uncertainty on paired differences. Use repeated seeds for sampled decoders and resample common document/record groups, recomputing micro counts per bootstrap draw. With only 9 clusters, intervals are fragile. More clusters are preferable to pretending every mention is independent. This adapts paired bootstrap reasoning from established evaluation work. [S11]
  6. Measure downstream utility separately. Evaluate graph-supported QA/retrieval with a controlled retriever and human-checked facts. Better QA may be useful despite stylistic extraction mismatches, but does not validate every extracted triple or replace the extraction experiment.

F4. Separate entity detection from grounded relation classification

Implement: First extract a source-grounded entity inventory with stable IDs. Evidence and limits: Only 1,757/3,437 gold directed surface pairs have both entity surfaces present in Qwen, and 501/3,437 have both types aligned. This is observed availability, not a universal recovery ceiling.

Evidence and limits: The current reference is a single model's unreviewed annotation. All 3,589 raw gold mentions occur in the source body, but this does not establish semantic correctness or completeness. S8 motivates independent review, not a claimed local error rate.

F7. Benchmark a GLiNER + GLiREL extraction pipeline

Implement: Use GLiNER for entity spans/types and GLiREL for relations given the predicted entities. Adapt outputs to the same benchmark contract and predefined ontology. Tune confidence cutoffs on development data only, then evaluate end to end on the same held-out passages.

Preserve integrity: Specialized NER may omit abstract concepts expected by this broad prompt. Align the task specification first; do not silently exclude unsupported categories or compare only a favorable subset.

Evidence and limits: These methods are supported on their authors' NER and relation-classification benchmarks, not demonstrated better on this corpus (S4-S5).

F8. Expose, pin and tune decoding/context on development data

Evidence and limits: Current provider code overrides Ollama's model temperature with 0 and does not pin num_ctx or seeds. Model context maximum is not evidence of allocated runtime context (S1-S3, S12).

F9. Task-adapt an extractor with LoRA on separate annotated training data

Preserve integrity: Never train on these 180 evaluation paragraphs, their answers, or paraphrases. Group splits by source article/document, not only question ID. Teacher-generated training data can transfer teacher style and errors; independent human evaluation remains necessary.

Evidence and limits: LoRA reduces adaptation cost; no data or experiment here supports a numerical gain or an optimal dataset size (S9).

F10. Evaluate deterministic invalid-output recovery as a separate condition

Implement: In a versioned postprocessing arm, retain valid mentions and reject only dangling triples when IDs cannot be resolved without guessing. Report raw validity, dropped items and recovered scores for every model using the same rule.

Preserve integrity: Keep strict raw-output scoring primary. Do not silently repair labels, invent IDs, drop difficult paragraphs or selectively retry Qwen. Preserve an audit of all original and postprocessed outputs.

Evidence and limits: The sole remaining failed paragraph references nonexistent M20. Even perfect replacement would reach only 38.45% entity and 4.08% relation F1, so schema recovery cannot explain most of the weak score.

Audit method, reproducibility and limits

  • Validated both v2 runs with existing artifact loaders, then checked the exact cohort, frozen reference and fingerprint match.
  • Independently recomputed TP/FP/FN and P/R/F1 from mention text/type and resolved triple endpoints for all 180 rows; agreed with the official scorer and stored scores within 1e-12.
  • Renamed all mention IDs and reordered mentions/triples for every reference result. Self-comparison stayed exact, demonstrating IDs and array positions do not enter the semantic keys.
  • Computed diagnostic projections by removing fields symmetrically from gold and predictions; preserved per-paragraph set deduplication. Conditional relation-label metrics use only shared directed pairs. These projections are not interchangeable primary metrics.
  • Hashed every file in both run directories before and after the audit; all bytes remained identical. Stored source hashes and scoring/provider code hashes in evidence.json.
  • Reviewed all 12 fixed-seed sample paragraphs, plus explicitly targeted examples. This is an exploratory assistant review, not a calibrated human audit, a blinded study, an estimated error rate or a causal model-quality comparison.
  • Queried localhost /api/version, /api/tags, /api/show and /api/ps for metadata only. No models were loaded or run; /api/ps was empty. Current metadata cannot prove historical run digests.
